Our Story

Why We Formed AVS in 2008

Audio, Video & Security, Inc. is owned and operated by Mike Sheffield, Cris Sheffield, and Steve Boyd. Mike, Cris, and Steve have a strong background in the security and low-voltage industry and wanted to provide their own unique level of personal customer service to their clients.

That is why they formed AVS Systems in 2008 right here in Valdosta, Georgia. Their many years of hands-on field experience, passion for the industry, and drive to stay on the cutting edge of technology make AVS the best choice for your residential and commercial audio, video, and security needs.
